Deepwarren

A free online co-op Android demo inside an immense, changing fungal underworld.

Public release preparation is in progress. The download will appear here after installation and server-capacity checks pass.

Android & Thor

Install the APK and open the game. It connects automatically to our server. No GPU, model download, VPN or server configuration is needed on your device.

Play on Windows or Mac

Our guided setup will configure an Android emulator, install the game and open its two displays. Start with Android Studio from Google; no development project is needed.

Mac support is being prepared and has not yet been verified on a physical Mac. Check Google's computer requirements.

A shared online world

Internet access is required. When all player slots are occupied, the game shows your queue position and admits you when a space opens. Hosting is provided by the developer's PC; availability may vary.
